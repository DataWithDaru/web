With Daru’s contract coming to an end this week, I’d like to acknowledge his contribution over the past year and thank him for the expertise, commitment, and support he’s brought to the team. Daru Broodryk joined us through FIRN and has made a significant contribution to the growth and maturity of our data platform. Some of his key contributions include:

  • Data ModellingA key Data Engineer across all releases, supporting development, testing and production deployment.
  • Data Protection & ClassificationLed the implementation of our data protection and classification capability, including governance artefacts.
  • Platform MigrationSupported a pilot migration, helping ensure successful reconciliation and deployment outcomes.
  • Data Engineering & Platform DevelopmentBuilt data models, reusable engineering patterns and governance controls that continue to underpin the platform.

  • Knowledge Transfer & InnovationConsistently shared knowledge through documentation, handovers and workshops, while introducing new ideas in areas such as automation, synthetic data and platform governance.

Throughout his time with us, Daru combined strong technical capability with a practical, collaborative approach. His work has left a lasting impact on our platform, governance capabilities and ways of working. I have personally really enjoyed working with Daru and wish him every success in his next chapter.
